# Formal Takedown / Cease-and-Desist Notice (Template)

INSTRUCTIONS: Replace every ALL-CAPS placeholder with case-specific details. This is a template for submitting to platform support, counsel, or law enforcement. It is not legal advice.

Date: DATE

To: PLATFORM NAME / Support Team
Re: Urgent removal request — Harassment, Privacy violation, and disclosure of sensitive information

1. Sender (Victim / Complainant)
- Name: [REDACTED NAME]
- Username / Account: [REDACTED USERNAME]
- Contact email: [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com]
- Phone (optional): [YOUR_PHONE]

2. Alleged Offending Account or Content
- Offending account: [OFFENDING_ACCOUNT_HANDLE]
- Offending profile URL or post URL(s): [OFFENDING_URL_1], [OFFENDING_URL_2]
- Date(s) and time(s) observed: [POST_DATE_TIMES]

3. Nature of the Complaint
I am submitting this request because the above-referenced content and/or account is engaging in targeted harassment and is linking my identity with another person/email in a manner that is false, harmful, and intended to harass and intimidate me. The content also includes disclosure of private information that is sensitive (POTENTIALLY HIPAA/medical-related). The continued availability of this material causes ongoing harm, risk to safety, and reputational damage.

4. Facts (Concise and Specific)
- FACT 1: The offending post(s) explicitly state or imply an association between [REDACTED USERNAME] and [OTHER IDENTIFIER] without consent.
- FACT 2: The content contains private information (describe without reproducing highly sensitive content here) that was disclosed without authorization.
- FACT 3: The poster(s) have engaged in repeated harassment [OR: is impersonating my account], dated [DATES], and have ignored direct requests to remove the material.

5. Requested Action
- Remove the identified content and, where appropriate, suspend or disable the offending account.
- Preserve all metadata and server logs associated with the offending account and content, including timestamps, IP addresses, and any communications related to the account, pending further legal process.

6. Legal Basis / Policy Basis (Platform-specific)
- This material violates your terms of service regarding harassment/abuse, impersonation, and privacy/PHI disclosure. It may also violate applicable law regarding privacy and harassment. Please process this as a high-priority removal request.

7. Good Faith Statement
I certify, under penalty of perjury under the laws of the applicable jurisdiction, that the information in this notice is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge and that I am authorized to submit this complaint. I request prompt removal of the material and preservation of evidence.

8. Evidence Attached
- Screenshots (full-resolution) showing the content, username, and timestamps.
- Direct URLs to offending posts/profiles.
- Copies of any direct messages or comments that demonstrate harassment.

9. Contact for Follow-up
Please send confirmation and any case number to: [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com]. If an expedited channel is available for privacy/medical disclosures, please escalate to that team.

10. Preservation Request Language (for legal preservation)
Please preserve all logs, communications, and account data for the offending account(s) referenced above, including but not limited to: account creation metadata, associated email addresses and phone numbers used for registration, IP address logs, and device identifiers. This preservation is requested for potential legal proceedings.

11. Signature

Sincerely,

[REDACTED NAME]

---

Notes & Tips:
- When submitting to Facebook/Instagram, choose "Harassment", "Privacy", or "Impersonation" as applicable and paste relevant sections above into the "Additional information" box.
- If PHI is exposed, include the phrase "Protected Health Information / HIPAA concern — urgent removal requested" to escalate.
- Keep copies of all submissions and support response case numbers.
