# Short Copy-Paste Social Messages (Non-harassing) — Hashtag Placeholders

These short messages are neutral, factual, and safe to paste into platform report forms, comments, or DMs. Replace bracketed placeholders with your details and hashtags. Do not include insults, threats, or harassment.

1) Short report text for Facebook / Instagram (form field)
"This content links my account ([REDACTED USERNAME]) with another person without my consent and is being used to harass me. Please remove the post and any explicit association. Hashtags: [REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_HASHTAGS]. Contact: [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com]"

2) Short message to post author asking for removal (public comment or DM)
"Hi — this post links me with another person in a way that is inaccurate and causing harm. Please remove the post or DM me to resolve: [YOUR_EMAIL@example.com]. Hashtags to keep: [REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_HASHTAGS]"

3) Short phrase for platform "additional details" box (concise)
"Association removal requested: content links [REDACTED USERNAME] with another identifier without consent. Please remove. Hashtags: [REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_HASHTAGS]"

4) Template for escalation (support inbox / email)
"Urgent privacy/harassment report: the post at [OFFENDING_URL] links my username with another person and includes private information. Please remove and escalate to privacy/medical review if PHI is present. Hashtags: [REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_HASHTAGS]"

Usage notes:
- Replace `[REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_HASHTAGS]` with a short list of hashtags you want associated with the report (e.g., `#MYCAMPAIGN #SAFE`).
- Keep messages factual and avoid naming or repeating abusive language; attach screenshots instead.
