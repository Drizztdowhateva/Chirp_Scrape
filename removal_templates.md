# Removal & Reporting Templates

Use these templates to report or request removal of mentions, posts, or accounts. Replace the placeholders (in ALL CAPS) with your details. Adapt tone and evidence as needed.

## 1) Platform Report Template (public post)

Subject: Request to remove defamatory/unauthorized content

Hello,

I am writing to request removal of the following content which refers to me (NAME / USERNAME) and is false/defamatory/unauthorized.

- Platform: PLATFORM_NAME
- URL(s): POST_URL_1, POST_URL_2
- Posted by (username/profile): POSTER_USERNAME
- Date(s): DATE

Why removal is requested:
- BRIEFLY EXPLAIN (false statements, privacy violation, impersonation, harassment, revealing private info, etc.)

Evidence attached:
- SCREENSHOTS / COPIES / LINKS / OTHER

Requested action:
- Please remove the content and, if possible, suspend the offending account. I am happy to provide additional evidence.

Contact information:
- NAME
- EMAIL
- PHONE (optional)

Thank you,

NAME

---

## 2) DMCA Copyright Takedown (if content infringes your copyright)

Use this only if you own the copyrighted material being shared without permission.

To: PLATFORM DMCA / Copyright Agent

I, the undersigned, state under penalty of perjury that I am the owner (or authorized representative) of the copyrighted work described below and that the information in this notification is accurate.

- Description of copyrighted work: DESCRIPTION
- Location of infringing material: INFRINGING_URL
- My contact info: NAME, EMAIL, ADDRESS

I have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner.

I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which my address is located, or if my address is outside the U.S., the district where the service provider may be found.

Signed,

NAME (typed)

---

## 3) Impersonation / Account Takeover Report (when someone is impersonating you)

Subject: Impersonation report — account impersonating NAME

Hello,

Account: OFFENDING_ACCOUNT
Profile URL: OFFENDING_PROFILE_URL

This account is impersonating me / my organization. I am attaching proof of identity: GOVT_ID OR OFFICIAL WEBSITE SCREENSHOT / OTHER.

Please remove or disable the account and advise next steps.

Thank you,

NAME

---

## 4) Short Public Reply Template (ask poster to remove)

Hi @POSTER_USERNAME — this post contains incorrect/private information about me. Please remove or DM me to resolve: CONTACT_EMAIL. Thank you.

---

## 5) Evidence Checklist (what to attach)

- Direct URL(s) to post/profile
- Full-resolution screenshots showing timestamp, username, and content
- Copies of messages if harassment is via DMs
- Proof of identity (for impersonation): government ID plus matching profile evidence
- Copyright ownership proof (for DMCA): registration or original files with timestamps

---

Save a record of every report (confirmation emails, case numbers, screenshots) and track follow-ups.
