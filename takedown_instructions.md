# Step-by-step Takedown & Reporting Instructions (Facebook / Meta)

These instructions cover common Meta properties (Facebook, Instagram). Use the templates in `removal_templates.md` to populate form fields.

1) Identify and save evidence
- Copy the direct URL to the post or profile.
- Take full screenshots showing username, timestamp, and the content.
- Save any direct messages if harassment was via DM.

2) Report the specific post (Facebook web)
- Open the post -> click the three-dot menu (⋯) -> "Find support or report post".
- Choose the reason: "Harassment or hate", "False information", "Privacy violation", or "Impersonation" as applicable.
- Follow prompts and upload screenshots if allowed.

3) Report an account (impersonation, harassment)
- Visit the impersonating profile -> click the three-dot menu -> "Find support or report profile" -> choose "Pretending to be someone" or other reason.
- For impersonation, Meta may ask for proof of identity (government ID). Use the impersonation template.

4) Use the Help Center forms (recommended for follow-up)
- Facebook report form: https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/ (choose the appropriate contact form)
- Instagram impersonation: https://help.instagram.com/368191326593075
- If the issue is a privacy/explicit image, use the specific removal forms listed in Meta Help Center.

5) For copyrighted content (DMCA)
- Use the platform's copyright form (Facebook/Instagram have DMCA submission pages). Attach the completed DMCA template (see `removal_templates.md`).

6) Escalation and support inbox
- After filing reports, check your Support Inbox at https://www.facebook.com/support/ for status updates.
- Save any case numbers or confirmation emails.

7) If content remains and is defamatory or illegal
- Consider a legal takedown letter or contacting a lawyer (template: short defamation notice). Keep records of harm and evidence.

8) Optional: use platform ads/appeals
- If a post is reported but remains, use the "appeal" options shown in the Support Inbox or the report confirmation.

9) Blocking & privacy controls (immediate mitigation)
- Block offending accounts to limit exposure.
- Tighten privacy settings: make profile posts visible to friends only; review tagged posts; enable manual review of tags.

10) Tracking and escalation checklist
- Record: date reported, platform form used, case number, screenshots, and response time.
- If no response after 72 hours, re-submit and escalate via appeals or business support if you have a paid ad account.
