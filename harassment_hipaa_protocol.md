# Harassment + HIPAA Protection Protocol

Use this protocol when reporting harassment, doxxing, or exposure of protected health information (PHI). Replace placeholders with case-specific details.

1) Preserve evidence (do this immediately)
- Save direct URLs to offending posts/profiles.
- Take full-resolution screenshots showing username, timestamp, and content (include browser chrome if available).
- Export or screenshot any DMs, comments, or message threads involved.
- If possible, record the HTML page (Save Page As) or use an archiving service (e.g., archive.org) to preserve content.

2) Mark content as PHI / privacy-sensitive when reporting
- On Facebook/Instagram report forms choose reasons like "Privacy violation" or "Harassment" and explicitly state the post reveals medical or other protected information (PHI).
- If a platform offers a dedicated channel for privacy/medical exposures, use it and include the words: "Protected Health Information / HIPAA concern — urgent removal requested."  

3) Ready-to-copy statement for platform forms
"This content discloses protected health information and personal medical details about me (NAME / username KATIE.FEKETE.5) without consent and is being used to harass and expose me. This is a HIPAA-sensitive privacy violation. Please remove the content immediately and suspend the offending account. Contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com"

4) Report channels and escalation
- File the platform report (Facebook/Instagram in-app/web form). Use the dedicated privacy/medical reporting options where available.
- If the content shows clear intent to harass or threaten, report to local law enforcement and provide the preserved evidence.
- If you have a legal representative, send a formal takedown/cease-and-desist letter and a DMCA notice if copyright applies.

5) Mitigation steps (immediate)
- Block offending accounts and set your accounts to private.
- Change passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and review connected apps.
- If contacts are receiving unwanted messages, notify them and ask them to delete or report the content.

6) Tracking and documentation
- Record: date reported, platform form used, case number, screenshots, and responses.
- If no response within 48–72 hours for PHI, escalate via platform business support or legal counsel.

7) Sample escalation note for platform support inbox
"Urgent: PHI disclosure and harassment — please remove. The post at OFFENDING_URL reveals protected health information about NAME and is being used to harass. Evidence attached. This requires immediate removal per your privacy policies. Case opened: CASE_ID (if any)."

8) Safety resources
- If harassment threatens physical safety, contact local authorities immediately.
- If the victim is experiencing mental health crises, contact local emergency services or crisis lines.
